
## Organizer note — keep out of the starting card

Version 1 includes original paper links and short source cards with labelled paraphrases and original excerpts. The cards permit limited offline checking, not a full-paper review. Do not upload this organizer note to the participant's agent.

Use these two previously checked original sources:

- [Zabelina & Silvia (2020), Percolating ideas](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32086187/):
  randomized caffeine/placebo comparison; benefit in problem solving, no statistically
  demonstrated benefit in creative idea generation. Original abstract is accessible;
  a reliably downloadable free full-text copy remains unconfirmed.
- [Stepan, Altmann & Fenn (2021), Caffeine selectively mitigates cognitive deficits caused by sleep deprivation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12884582/):
  caffeine/placebo with sleep/sleep-deprivation conditions; benefits for vigilant
  attention did not extend to placekeeping for most participants. Full text accessible.

Give teams the actual original abstracts or appropriate original sections with
citations, not only facilitator paraphrases. They need not read both full papers
within the station. This is bounded evidence synthesis, not a systematic review.

Neither study measures time to manuscript acceptance, coffee-machine quality or
return on a purchase. Caffeine administration and coffee consumption also differ.
Do not announce these gaps as the answer before teams draft their paragraph.
Do not seek a predetermined pro- or anti-coffee conclusion or score skepticism.
