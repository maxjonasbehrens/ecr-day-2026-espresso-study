# What did we hand over? · release at 14:10

Look at your actual result and conversation. Pick two decisions built into the output.

1. Who made each decision: us, the agent, or both? Include facilitator input.
2. Did we notice it at the time, or only afterwards?
3. Would we delegate it the same way next time? Why?

Keeping the agent's choice can be deliberate and useful. You do not need to find a mistake or change something to succeed.

For your station report: “The agent decided ___. We noticed when ___. Next time we would ___.”
