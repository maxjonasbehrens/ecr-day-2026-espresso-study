
## Organizer note — keep out of the starting card

Version 1 results figure and note are generated from the same dataset as Stations 1 and 2.
Supply a verified prepared result rather than waiting for Station 2's live output.
The numerical pattern must come from the same synthetic study used at the other
stations. Do not pick a separate result to make a preferred purchasing decision.

The results note states group sizes, events, follow-up, what each curve
represents, and which analyses were actually performed. It should supply enough
information to interpret the figure without making participants guess missing
study facts. Do not include a ready-made purchasing conclusion.

This station translates the trial results into a recommendation. Station 3 writes
the external scientific background; Station 4 need not independently review its
papers. No real communication or purchase takes place. Keep that disclosure, but clarify
the role: recommendations should address the study's evidence within the story,
not dismiss the exercise because the data are simulated. Supply factual results
and definitions, not the intended purchasing argument. A factual note can prime
an answer too if it already explains why the machine is or is not justified.
