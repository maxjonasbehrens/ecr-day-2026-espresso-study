# Mentimeter slides — set up before the session

Free plan: 50 participants. Answer **one device per team** on both slides; with
teams of 2–4 this covers up to about 100 people. Test with your own device only.
Put the Menti code on the projector and replace `MENTI_CODE` in `site/build.py`
if you want it on the website too.

## Slide 1 · Open ended · 14:10–14:20

Question: **Which decision did you leave to the agent?**

Subtitle: “Station ___: we left ___ to the agent. Next time we would ___.”

Settings: several answers per participant off; show results as a list, not a
word cloud; hide results until 14:15. At 14:15 read one or two answers per
station and ask the room for a short reaction.

## Slide 2 · 2×2 grid · 14:20–14:28

Question: **Where would you place each station's task?**

Items (prepared): Station 1 · Prepare the data; Station 2 · Show the results;
Station 3 · Find the evidence; Station 4 · Write the reply.

Axis x: “Result is hard to check” → “Result is easy to check”
Axis y: “I would decide myself” → “I would delegate again”

Each team places all four stations, including those it did not work on. Show
the averaged grid and ask: where did the room agree, where did it split?

## Closing slide "Take home" (no interaction) · 14:28–14:30

Question: “What will you hand to an agent, and what will you keep for yourself?”

Line below: “Agents can make many of the choices, not just do the work. Notice
which ones you hand over.”

Ask the question aloud, allow 30 seconds of silence, then read the line.
