# Results: fictional coffee study

The study randomly assigned 30 researchers to caffeinated coffee and 30 to decaffeinated coffee. Follow-up lasted 365 days from randomization. The event was first manuscript acceptance, not submission or online publication.
All records were generated for this workshop using random allocation. The simulation assumes effects; it does not estimate them from the caffeine papers.
Kaplan-Meier curves estimate the proportion still waiting for acceptance. Lower curves indicate earlier acceptance. Shading shows an approximate 95% confidence interval for each curve, calculated with the log-log Greenwood method. Plus signs mark censoring. The marks overlap on day 365.
All censoring occurs when follow-up ends on day 365. The analysis does not include a log-rank test, hazard ratio, adjusted comparison, confidence interval for the group difference or cost analysis.

| Group | Randomized | Accepted by day 365 | Still waiting/censored | Estimated median days to acceptance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| caffeinated | 30 | 19 | 11 | 176 |
| decaffeinated | 30 | 19 | 11 | 226 |

Participants still waiting were included until day 365.
